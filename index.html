Educational Autonomy

What is a Personal Learning Environment (PLE)?

A Personal Learning Environment gives YOU full control over how you absorb knowledge. Instead of forcing everyone to read identical textbook lines, our ICT platform lets you choose whether to WATCH visual animations, LISTEN to audio stories, or PLAY algorithm quests!

Autonomy & Choice

Select any lesson format that feels natural to your brain today. Switch modes anytime without losing progress!

Multi-Modal Learning

Combine visual block diagrams, radio podcasts, and interactive code quests for deeper understanding.

Self-Paced Growth

Zero discrimination and zero anxiety. Master searching algorithms at your own speed with full confidence.

Curriculum Pathways

Choose Your Algorithm Learning Method

Explore how Linear Search and Binary Search are taught across three distinct interactive pathways.

Visual Pathway

Visual Diagram & Animation Path

Perfect for students who love graphics! See arrays split in half during Binary Search, watch pointers step through elements in Linear Search, and view flowcharts.

  • Color-coded array visualizers
  • Flowchart decision diamonds
  • Video explainers & diagrams
Audio Pathway

Podcast Path

Ideal for auditory learners! Listen to radio-style podcasts where teachers explain searching using analogies like searching for books in the School Library.

  • Library book story podcast
  • Spoken algorithmic logic breakdowns
  • Audio voice-note reflection tasks
Game Pathway

Interactive Relic Quest Game

Built for kinesthetic learners! Play through the quest. Click chest boxes to test why Binary Search takes far fewer steps than Linear Search.

  • Interactive 7-chest puzzle game
  • Live step counter & score tracker
  • Hands-on efficiency comparison
